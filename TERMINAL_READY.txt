EXACT BINDING PROFILE ADMISSION

The profile materializer accepts only Spiderweb-certified rows with
Uncertainty_Class=EXACT and one of:
COVERS
INTERSECTS_POSITIVE_AREA
LINE_POSITIVE_LENGTH

INTERSECTS_POSITIVE_AREA additionally requires positive Overlap_Area_m2 and
Cell_Coverage_Fraction > 0 and <= 1.
LINE_POSITIVE_LENGTH additionally requires positive Length_In_Cell_m.

ADMINISTRATIVE_SET, DERIVED_SET, APPROXIMATE, INFERRED, UNKNOWN and all other
non-exact rows fail closed and cannot enter exact Cell_Profile counts.
Geometry_Included remains false; canonical geometry stays in spiderweb-pr.

FAIL-CLOSED / RETENTION RULE
If data/usgs_monitoring_locations.jsonl is unavailable, the exporter exits non-zero
and does not overwrite the last good candidate or receipt. The refresh step is
optional so an upstream USGS outage does not erase the previous verified snapshot.
Duplicate Record_ID values fail closed. Conflicting coordinates fail closed.

SCHEDULE
The candidate exporter runs immediately after STEP_USGS_METADATA on daily, weekly,
and all refresh plans. It is intentionally absent from fast cadence.

AUTHORITY BOUNDARY
A candidate row is not a V2 binding and does not establish a Cell_ID. Spiderweb
must consume the frozen candidate bytes, verify the candidate receipt, transform
EPSG:4326 to EPSG:6566, and emit the authoritative binding receipt before any
Cell_Profile uses the record.
